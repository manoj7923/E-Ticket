Project Heritage & Vision

Empowering Millions of Commuters Across WR, CR & HR Lines

This platform is a next-generation suburban train ticketing system inspired by Indian Railways CRIS UTS (Unreserved Ticketing System) and IRCTC. Architected and built by Manoj Gupta as a flagship college project, it modernizes ticketing across Mumbai's massive suburban rail network.

It features interactive transit map route booking, multi-line fare calculation, interchange routing at Dadar and Kurla, and strict 12-hour ticket validity enforcement with real-time countdown meters.

Core Platform Features

  • Unified 3-Line Network: Western (124 km), Central (121 km), Harbour (53 km).
  • Interactive Transit Map: Point-and-click route selection with SVG track tracing.
  • 12-Hour Ticket Validity: Real-time countdown timer & progress meter.
  • Smart Interchange Routing: Automatic transfers at Dadar (WR ⇄ CR) and Kurla (CR ⇄ HR).
